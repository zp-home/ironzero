fix(bridge): exclude unfinished factories and protect builders in all tactical orders

1. isOperational() now returns false for a factory building that is not built.
   Previously an unfinished landFactory reported is_operational=true (full HP,
   buildProgress=0) and the curriculum's _combat_factories accepted it as a
   working factory. That is why probe_fast_ground saw peak_army=0 with two
   "landFactory" entities standing: no production could ever start.

2. attack_target and holdGround exclude builders from their unit selection,
   matching the exclusion already applied to move_group/attack_move. A trained
   argmax policy issued attack_target on 1851/2000 decisions with group=0 -- the
   builder's group -- which repeatedly cancelled in-flight construction.

3. Action masks now also close attack_target while a structure is under
   construction (it addresses a group that contains the builder through
   id % GROUP_COUNT, so it re-tasks the builder in practice).

Verified: 151 core tests pass. With the rebuilt jar, the d1_attack_fixed
checkpoint on Lake d1 recovers from army=0 / no attack orders to peak 16 combat
units, 789 curriculum attack_move orders, and income 42.

Also adds exploratory probes for air-factory tech tree, fast ground rushes across
6 2p maps, AI-pilot baseline and attack cadence. Measured:
  - built-in AI pilot vs d1 also ends in timeout at 30000 ticks (both sides intact)
  - fast ground rush (attack_at=6, 2 factories, 0 turrets, reserve 350) wins on
    Lake 1/8 seeds and Dire_Straight 2/2, but loses most others: the remaining
    bottleneck is enemy air superiority (lightGunship x4-9 by tick 15000).
  - hoverTank is amphibious and can cross Lake; airFactory produces helicopter
    with 150hp, 130 range, 1.8 speed, can_attack_air+land.

Objective still not achieved: best reproducible difficulty-1 Lake win rate on
